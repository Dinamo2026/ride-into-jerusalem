✦ The secret of the bike ✦

See More of Jerusalem
in a Few Hours

On foot, these places take days and a lot of tired feet. On a bike, the whole city opens up in just a few hours, morning or afternoon, at an easy pace, with time to stop at every view.

  • Jaffa Gate & the Old City walls
  • Stone alleys of the Old City
  • Views of the Temple Mount & the Dome of the Rock
  • The Mount of Olives at sunset
  • The ancient tombs of the Kidron Valley
  • The Yemin Moshe windmill
  • The suspension bridge
  • The colorful streets of downtown

Every route is designed for your family, so the stops change from journey to journey.
